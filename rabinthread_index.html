About RABIN THREAD & ACCESSORIES

Manufacturer & Exporter • Established 2017

One-stop accessories solutions

RABIN THREAD & ACCESSORIES started its journey in 2017 and has developed as a garments accessories manufacturer and supplier in Bangladesh. The company focuses on quality, flexible orders, shorter lead times and dependable service.

We provide accessories and supply-chain solutions designed to support garment manufacturers and brands.
